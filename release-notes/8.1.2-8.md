## Common

- Fix `-shortest` with video and audio from separate inputs holding up to 10 s of video frames
  - On QSV with the Intel Media SDK runtime (API before 2.9), this failed about 10 s in with `Can't allocate a surface` or `Failed to query an output frame`
- Fix hardware-decoded streams failing with `Impossible to convert between the formats supported by the filter` and `Error reinitializing filters!` when the input's color tags change mid-stream, such as in spliced or smart-cut files
  - After the change, the output keeps the color tags it started with; software frames are still converted
- QSV: fix encoding failing with `Error submitting the frame for encoding` and `Internal bug, should not have happened` after the filter graph is rebuilt mid-stream on the Intel Media SDK runtime (API before 2.9)
